Troop 322 has been chartered since 1971. Since 2019, Troop 322 has been chartered by the Dickson City Fire Department.

Each spring, Troop 322 conducts a traveling camping trip outside of our local Council, past trips have included Washington, D.C., Cooperstown- New York, Valley Forge- Pennsylvania, Baltimore- Maryland, and Gettysburg- Pennsylvania.

Since 1971, over 50 Troop members have earned the Eagle Scout Award.

Troop 322 consistently excels at District level Scouting competitions, including Klondike Derbies and First Aid Meets.
